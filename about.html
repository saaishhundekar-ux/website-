Our Story

Quality First, Always

ISIRI is dedicated to producing superior quality seeds for its clients. We combine decades of production expertise with local growing conditions to deliver seeds that meet global standards.

Every plot during the seed production process goes through rigorous quality checks to ensure purity, performance, and consistency — and we work closely with growers to understand their evolving needs.

Based in Davanagere, Karnataka, ISIRI specializes in hybrid vegetable seed production — including tomato, chilli, watermelon, gourd, and cucumber — grown in net houses under contract farming with close supervision.

Led by a founder with over 30 years of seed production experience and supported by a dedicated team of agronomists and field quality personnel, ISIRI has grown from a local seed production initiative into a trusted partner for clients across India, and now reaching many overseas market.
